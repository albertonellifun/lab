How to play

Every cat is territorial: it wants a home of its own, far from the others.

  • One row, one cat. Every row in the grid holds exactly one.
  • One column, one cat. Same rule, vertically.
  • One colored area, one cat. Every territory holds exactly one.
  • Never touching. Not even diagonally: cats need their space.
  • Tap to mark. First tap: X (ruled out). Second: cat. Third: empty.
  • 3 lives. Every cat placed in the wrong cell costs one.
